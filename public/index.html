MADE FOR THE MOMENTS BETWEEN

Not another wall of data.
A better next step.

Designed to help people make sense of changing conditions together, without asking them to become data analysts first.

01 / SEE

Signals, in context

Bring readings, rainfall, and the places they relate to into one shared view—not a scattered set of tabs.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Changes that stand out

Use readable trends and configurable thresholds to help a team decide which locations need a closer look.

03 / COORDINATE

Local knowledge included

Give community observations a place beside sensor data, with clear status and a simple way to follow up.
